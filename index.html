About the Hotel

Welcome
to Kadrisa Hotel

Our hotel is located in Durrës, one of Albania's oldest cities and its most popular Adriatic beach destination, just a short drive from Tirana. Comfortable, modern rooms combine everyday convenience with the warmth of a Mediterranean home.

  • ✓ Private beach access, just a 2-minute walk
  • ✓ Terrace and restaurant with panoramic sea views
  • ✓ Home-style cooking with local produce and our own olive oil
  • ✓ Intimate atmosphere — only 24 rooms, personal service
