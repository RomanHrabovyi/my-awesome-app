Welcome
to Kadrisa Hotel

Our hotel is located in Durrës, one of Albania's oldest cities and its most popular Adriatic beach destination, just a short drive from Tirana. Comfortable, modern rooms combine everyday convenience with the warmth of a Mediterranean home.

  • ✓ Just 250 m from the beach
  • ✓ On-site restaurant and bar serving Mediterranean dishes
  • ✓ Cozy lounge and garden terrace to unwind
  • ✓ Double, twin, and family rooms to fit any group
